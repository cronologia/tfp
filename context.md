## The subject

**The TFP** — Sociedade Brasileira de Defesa da Tradição, Família e
Propriedade, founded São Paulo, 26 July 1960, by the lay Catholic leader
**Plinio Corrêa de Oliveira** (1908–1995): youngest constituent deputy
(1933), Legionário director, author of *Revolução e Contra-Revolução*
(1959). Milestones: *Reforma Agrária: Questão de Consciência* (1960, with
bishops Sigaud and Castro Mayer), the street campaigns, the 1968 petition
(movement's own numbers), the international network (US 1973, France 1977,
Poland 1995/99), the April 1985 CNBB note, the post-1995 succession dispute
→ Heralds of the Gospel (1999, pontifical approval 22 Feb 2001, name won in
court 2003–04, apostolic visitation 2017, commissioner rejected 2019,
schools closed 2021) vs the IPCO (2006, founders' line).
